This is an article-pattern synthetic panel, not original participant data. It uses a simulated effect within the article's reported range, plus assumed wind/exposure relationships, subject baselines, and measurement noise. The held-out synthetic-subject R² is negative even with PM2.5 and wind as inputs, so the model is a weak fit to this simulated panel—not an individual forecast or a medical/clean-air route recommendation.
