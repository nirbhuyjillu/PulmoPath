Understanding these results

PM2.5 is fine particulate matter that can reach deep into the lungs. Wind and air-quality forecasts are regional model estimates, not personal exposure measurements.

Peak flow measures airflow, not oxygen levels or carbon dioxide release. Follow your clinician-provided asthma action plan; do not use this experimental model to choose treatment or delay urgent care.

Driving directions do not account for smoke along the route, visibility, closures, or evacuation orders. Check official alerts before travel.
